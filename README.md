# Eevee Portfolio — Titles & Research Restored

Static, single-page personal portfolio for college applications.

## Sections
About Me • Meet Jasper • Little Universe • K-pop Club • Dance • Research • Alpine.

Every major area has a visible main title. K-pop photos, UCLA lab photos, and Alpine photos also have clearly styled photo-section headings. The UCLA written research report and 19 numbered preparation/testing steps are restored in the Research section. The external PDF button has been removed as requested.

## How to use
- To deploy, upload this folder (or the ZIP containing it) to Netlify.
- Open index.html in a browser for local viewing. YouTube links may not embed when using file://; Netlify hosting is preferred.
- Keep assets/, app.js, gallery-data.js, styles.css and cinematic.css with index.html.

## Content
90 original project/gallery photos plus newer Remembrance Day performance photos and an Eevee portrait, five YouTube links, full UCLA lab report.
